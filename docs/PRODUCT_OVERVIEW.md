## Executive Brief, Mission, System Capabilities & Value Proposition

**Platform Name:** Core Strategy & Intelligence Platform (CSIP)  
**System Architecture:** Local-First Decision Intelligence & Machine Learning Platform  
**Target Release:** Definitive Consolidated Release (v1.0.0-Definitive)  
**Certification Standard:** SKKNI 299/2020 (Kepmenaker No. 299 Tahun 2020)  
**Scheme:** LSP TRI — Sertifikasi Ilmuwan Data (*Data Scientist*), KKNI Level 7 (`SKM/2905/LSP-TRI/VII/2025`)  

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE MISSION & IDENTITY

The **Core Strategy & Intelligence Platform (CSIP)** is an enterprise-grade Data Science platform designed to transform raw transactional e-commerce data into auditable, scientifically validated decision intelligence. Built upon 99,441 real-world orders from the Brazilian E-Commerce public dataset (Olist), CSIP bridges the gap between predictive machine learning, behavioral customer clustering, and macro-economic dynamic simulation.

CSIP is intentionally designed as an **applied Data Science platform, not a black-box AI wrapper or generic dashboard**. Every probability estimate, counterfactual scenario, and behavioral finding is traceable to clean data partitions, validated statistical methods, and reproducible open-source algorithms.

---

### 2. CORE PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

1. **Executive Overview & Key Insights (Chapter 1):**
   - High-level decision narratives communicating the core dilemma: customer forgiveness towards shipping costs vs delivery delays.
   - Strategic takeaways framed for C-level operators and logistics managers.

2. **Data Observatory & Semantic Profiler (Chapter 2):**
   - Automated profiling of tabular e-commerce distributions, missing value structures, and relational schemas.
   - Declarative data contract validation categorizing observations into clean, suspicious, and anomalous.

3. **Behavioral Discovery Lab (Chapter 3):**
   - Unsupervised customer segmentation utilizing K-Means on RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) vectors.
   - Silhouette analysis ($s = 0.418$) and elbow method validation for optimal cluster selection ($K=4$).

4. **Hypothesis Laboratory (Chapter 4):**
   - Rigorous non-parametric statistical hypothesis testing (Mann-Whitney U, Chi-Square, Fisher Exact).
   - Validating friction factors: delay vs review score ($p < 10^{-15}$), freight ratio sensitivity, and state-level disparities.

5. **Predictive Intelligence & Probability Calibration (Chapter 5):**
   - Supervised classification of customer dissatisfaction risk (`is_negative_review`) achieving holdout test ROC-AUC `0.6745` and calibrated Brier score `0.0762`.
   - Continuous delivery delay forecasting (`delivery_delay_days`) achieving test MAE `4.8256` days.
   - NLP sentiment classification via PyTorch BiLSTM neural checkpoint achieving macro-F1 `0.8420`.
   - Subgroup slice error analysis evaluating regional and order value disparities.

6. **Macroeconomic World Model (Chapter 6):**
   - Multivariate vector autoregression (Ridge VAR) modeling weekly system dynamics across order volume, delivery delay, and review sentiment.
   - Spectral radius and eigenvalue stability analysis ensuring non-explosive state transitions.

7. **Alternative World Simulator (Chapter 7):**
   - Monte Carlo counterfactual simulation evaluating policy interventions (e.g. freight subsidy vs routing speedup).
   - Simulating multi-week platform trajectories under localized logistical shocks.

8. **Decision Studio & ROI Calculator (Chapter 8):**
   - Interactive policy optimizer translating statistical risk scores into currency-denominated operational decisions (voucher costs vs customer lifetime value preservation).

9. **SKKNI / LSP TRI Competency Evidence Review (Chapter 9):**
   - Interactive competency evidence browser displaying all 63 KUKs across 11 Unit Kompetensi.
   - Direct traceability to source artifacts, verification procedures, and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes.
